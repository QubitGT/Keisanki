Inside the menu

Everything sits one tap from your hand

Six pages cover the whole menu. The info panel on the right explains whatever you point at before you press it.

MODS

Enabled Mods

A single list of every mod you have switched on. Check what is running before you join a lobby, and turn things off without digging through pages.

MODS

Favorite Mods

Pin the mods you reach for most so they sit at the top of the list.

ROOM

Room Mods

Tools that act on the room you are standing in, grouped together and clearly labeled.

Choose how the menu opens, which hand holds it, and how notifications appear on screen.

Install

Three steps to your wrist

  1. Install BepInEx

    Qubit runs as a BepInEx plugin. Put BepInEx into your Gorilla Tag folder and start the game once so it creates its folders.

  2. Drop in the plugin

    Download Qubit and copy the DLL into the plugins folder shown on the right.

  3. Launch and open the menu

    Start Gorilla Tag. The menu loads with the game and opens from the hand you chose in Settings.

Qubit for Gorilla Tag

Copy the plugin to this folder inside your game directory:

BepInEx/plugins/Qubit.dll
